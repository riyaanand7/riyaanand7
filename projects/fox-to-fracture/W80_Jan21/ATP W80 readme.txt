Mode: Web 
Sample: Subsample
Language: English and Spanish

***************************************************************************************************************************
NOTES

The W80 dataset contains the following variables for self-reported vote that were measured in the ATP W78 Post-Election survey:
VOTEGEN_POST_W78
VOTED_W78


For a small number of respondents with high risk of identification, certain values have been randomly swapped with those of lower risk cases with similar characteristics.


***************************************************************************************************************************
WEIGHTS 


WEIGHT_W80 is the weight for the sample. Data for all Pew Research Center reports are analyzed using this weight.
